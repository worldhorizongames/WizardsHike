# Privacy policy — Wizards Hike

_Last updated 9 August 2026_

Wizards Hike counts your steps to move your wizard along the journey. Everything
it records stays on your phone. The app has no internet access at all, so nothing
you do here can be sent anywhere.

## What the app reads

Your step count, and nothing else. It comes from Health Connect if you allow it,
or from your phone's built-in step sensor otherwise. The app also keeps the
choices you make along the way — your wizard's name, your companion's name and
species, your wand, friend, speciality, gift, mount and house — together with
your settings.

## Where it is kept

On your phone, in the app's own private storage: one step total per day since you
installed it, and your journey. No account is created and no server is involved.

## What is shared

Nothing. Wizards Hike does not request internet permission, and contains no
advertising, no analytics and no third-party component able to transmit anything.
Your data is never sold, shared, or sent to the developer.

## Reading steps in the background

The app asks Health Connect for permission to read your steps while it is closed.
Without it your wizard would only move while you had the app open. It is used for
nothing else, and you can withdraw it at any time in your phone's Health Connect
settings.

## Android backup

If Android backup is switched on for your phone, your step history and your
journey are saved to your own Google account, encrypted, so they follow you to
your next phone. Android handles this, not the app, and you control it from your
system settings. The step sensor's reference value stays on the device: it means
nothing anywhere else.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it kept on your phone. You can withdraw
its access to your steps whenever you like, in Health Connect or in your phone's
app permissions, and delete any backup copy from your Google account's backup
settings.

## Children

Wizards Hike knowingly collects nothing from anyone, at any age: there is no
account, no profile sent anywhere, and no data leaves the device.
